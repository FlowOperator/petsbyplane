Airlines & partners
Your consultant selects the best airline and route for your pet, weighing breed restrictions, temperature embargoes, transit hub conditions and seasonality — this isn't a self-serve flight picker.
British Airways
Hub: London Heathrow
One of the most-used carriers for pet transport, direct to 150+ destinations. Short brachycephalic (snub-nosed breed) restriction list, minimal seasonal temperature restrictions — useful for warm-climate destinations year-round.
Emirates
Hub: Dubai (DXB)
Wide-body aircraft, no weight/size restriction, no minimum charge — popular for eastbound routes (Australia, New Zealand via Dubai). Carries snub-nosed breeds in cooler months subject to a BOAS health assessment.
American Airlines
Direct to 9 major US cities from Heathrow
Popular for US-bound pets — wide-body aircraft, multiple daily departures. Does not accept snub-nosed breeds, and has summer temperature embargoes on some routes.
Lufthansa
Hub: Frankfurt
No snub-nosed breed restrictions, 100+ destinations both west and east. All pets transit through the EU via Frankfurt. Offers a premium pet service for an extra fee.
Qatar Airways
Hub: Doha (DOH)
Newly rebuilt reception centre in Doha. Restricts most snub-nosed breeds but will carry some other typically-restricted breeds in reinforced crates. Documents only needed at check-in.
KLM
Hub: Amsterdam (AMS)
Favourable rates and low minimums, 100+ destinations. Few restrictions on snub-nosed breeds with a BOAS assessment.
