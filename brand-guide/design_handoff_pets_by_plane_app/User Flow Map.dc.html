A brand-new install always opens on Welcome. Nobody is forced to sign in or sign up just to browse or get a quote — "Continue as guest" and "Skip" both go straight to Get a Quote. The account is only actually created at Checkout. Sign In (for returning users) looks up the existing account's hasBooking flag and routes into Lane 3a or 3b accordingly.
